About AfriYouth

We identify, nurture and advance promising ideas from Africa’s young innovators, through human-centered design, applied research and innovation.

Our mission

To empower Africa’s youth to generate evidence, shape policy, and develop innovative solutions to Africa’s most pressing socio-economic challenges through research, human-centered design, and entrepreneurship.

Our vision

An Africa where empowered young people use evidence, innovation and entrepreneurship to solve complex challenges, shape policies and drive inclusive, sustainable socio-economic transformation by 2050.
